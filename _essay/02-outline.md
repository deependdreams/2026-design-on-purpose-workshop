## Learning Intention

The workshop series aims to: 
- foster conversation and learning about metadata and data model design
- share varied frameworks and vocabularies around collections work
- explore metadata as a foundation for expressing collective values, goals and priorities
- analyze how different metadata structures shape what practices and approaches may be expressed
- create a space for sharing and responding to different digital exhibitions, collections, projects and spaces
- honor the knowledge and experience we all bring to the room
- hold space for listening, reflecting, rest and connection
- apply the above to the project! 
  
## Outline
 Throughout both days, there will be opportunities for sharing through talking, writing, doodling as well as through presence.
